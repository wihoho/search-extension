Welcome to OmniSearch!

Transform your browsing with instant search shortcuts

🚀 Getting Started

1

Type the trigger

In Chrome's address bar, type os

os |
2

Press Tab or Space

This activates OmniSearch mode

Search OmniSearch for: |
3

Enter your search

Type a keyword followed by your search term

Search OmniSearch for: youtube cats |
4

Press Enter

You'll be taken directly to the search results!

→ YouTube search for "cats"

💡 Try These Examples

os youtube how to cook pasta → Search YouTube
os youtube → Leave out the search words to just open the site
os you cats → Shortened keywords work too
os youtube+amazon headphones → Search several sites at once
os best pizza near me → No matching keyword? You get a normal web search

🎯 What's Next?

🔧 Customize Settings

Click the OmniSearch icon in the toolbar (or press Ctrl+Shift+S) to open the side panel. Add your own sites, pick from suggested ones, or turn off the ones you don't use.
