Welcome to OmniSearch!

Transform your browsing with instant search shortcuts

🚀 Getting Started

1

Type the trigger

In Chrome's address bar, type os

os |
2

Press Tab or Space

This activates OmniSearch mode

Search OmniSearch for: |
3

Enter your search

Type a keyword followed by your search term

Search OmniSearch for: youtube cats |
4

Press Enter

You'll be taken directly to the search results!

→ YouTube search for "cats"
